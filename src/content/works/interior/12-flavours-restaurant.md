The brief called for a relaxing and fresh Sichuan dining room. A myriad of references to Chinese architectural tradition are harnessed. The entrance takes its geometry from a Chinese eave façade. A bracket arch frames arrival. Inside, a traditional tile feature wall anchors the space with built in multi-drawer cabinet which runs along the main space. The ceiling is furnished with wood-grained aluminium strip panels, carrying Chinese lanterns reinterpreted in a contemporary form.

#### **Materials and Techniques**

* Materials: Natural wood, high quality metals, durable leather, and fabrics.
* Techniques: Advanced woodworking, precise metalwork, and modern lighting control systems.

#### **Sustainability and Environment**

* Eco-friendly Materials: Sustainably sourced wood and low VOC paints.
* Energy-efficient Design: Utilizes high efficiency LED lighting systems.
