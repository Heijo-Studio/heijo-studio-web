Comprehensive interior design for a Taikoo Shing residence focus on\
creating a harmonious blend of modern aesthetics and functionality.\
The project involves space planning, custom furniture design, lighting\
design, and the integration of smart home technology to enhance daily\
living. Special attention is given to maximizing space efficiency and\
ensuring a cohesive, elegant look throughout the home.
